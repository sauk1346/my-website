# Exercises

Covertir a 
- pasado simple afirmativo
- pasado simple negativo
- pregunta cerrada (yes/no)
- pregunta abierta 

1. **Mario jugó tenis**

<Solution title="Solución oración 1">
- Mario played tennis
- Mario did not play tennis
- Did Mario play tennis?
- Why did Mario play tennis?
</Solution>

2. **Mi padre trabajó ayer**

<Solution title="Solución oración 2">
- My dad worked yesterday
- My dad did not work yesterday
- Did may dad work yesterday?
- Where did my dad work yesterday?
</Solution>

3. **Fuimos a la playa**

<Solution title="Solución oración 3">
- We went to the beach
- We did not go to the beach
- Did we go to the beach?
- When did we go to the beach?
</Solution>

4. Yo y mi hermano caminamos ayer
<Solution title="Solución oración 4">
- Me and my brother walked yesterday
- Me and my brother did not walked yesterday
- Did me and my brother walk yesterday?
- Why did me and my brother walk yesterday?
</Solution>
